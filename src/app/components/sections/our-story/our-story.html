Our Story

From Zacatecas to Utah.

Mi Casa Tortilleria began in 2012 with a simple idea: bring our family together and share the flavors we grew up with in Zacatecas.

What started with fresh corn tortillas grew into a neighborhood place for traditional Mexican food, family recipes, and meals made to be shared.
